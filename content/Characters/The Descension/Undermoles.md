Most of the more primitive tribes have developed to the point of domesticating some animals.  The Tribes on the great Plains of the Empire use horses to travel and birds of prey to hunt. Other tribes use what animals are native to their homes. For the followers of the fallen gods, they have had little choice but the half wild undermoles are the closest they have. They are used to help build new tunnels and alert when danger is near. Unfortunately they are also known to chew through everything including equipment, housing and defences.

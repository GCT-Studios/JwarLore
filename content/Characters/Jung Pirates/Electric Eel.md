All manner of terrible and strange creatures live in the deep ocean where mortal man can never see. When the Sea Witches of the Miyakomo ask for aid the Kami can send these monstrous, snake-like fiends to assist the Jung. If a few fallen Korusea are consumed then that too is part of the price. These creatures can only venture into land when the sea comes with the Jung, otherwise Ryujin is content to let them shock sailors from beneath while Raijin and Fujin make lightning above.

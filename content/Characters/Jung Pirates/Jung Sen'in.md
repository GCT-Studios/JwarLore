Sen’in was around while the Jung were still a loyal Samurai clan and the official navy in the Jwar Isles. She was part of the events that lead to the clan being cast out and becoming pirates. She is alone in the Jung family in claiming the rank of Commodore, and the rest of her family respect the rank, even Hibiki has been known to salute Sen’in!  
She runs her ship properly and does not tolerate the usual pirate rabble. To help keep firm discipline she keeps a unit of specialised marines on board. They do not do work except under arms, they sleep and eat in separate parts of the ship and are loyal to Sen’in.

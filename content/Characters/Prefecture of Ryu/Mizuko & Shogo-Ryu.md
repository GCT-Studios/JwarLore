Mizuko had been a Shisai of Michi and her teaching included not only the Ancestors of her clan but also the Kami representing the Mon of the Clan. Her piety and the honour she showed to the clan's patron caused Shōgo-Ryu to go for a closer look. What the inscrutable dragons think of their mortal charges we can never know but this duo seems to be a formidable team, more than the sum of their parts.

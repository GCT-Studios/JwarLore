Ryokeen appeared with Koga one day as if he had always been there. A tiny puppy-like creature that was playful and friendly with Taiyo and the other children. [[Hozumi Daihanji|Daihanji]] was able to play with Ryokeen, and even [[Hozumi Maha|Maha]] took an interest in the child after she saw his new pet. Ryokeen, in fact, is a rare yokai; while he appeared like a small puppy early on, he grew to his current size quickly and indeed seems to still be growing. Ryokeen fixated on Koga early, and his nature is to find remarkable humans to be raised by. 

But Maha has discovered the truth and knows that Ryokeen will live for a very long time, as his temperament and nature were determined by his treatment early on in life. Ryokeen's life with Koga would set him up for a future of benevolent interactions with humans and assisting them as he continues to love and support his human friends over many centuries. Maha's allies among the Cult and elsewhere would like to see the creature corrupted to be a creature that will inflict pain and suffering on the humans who wronged him; centuries of being the monster at the door will give those who revel in fear and destruction a considerable edge in the region.

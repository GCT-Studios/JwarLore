---
title: Yūkan'na, the Prince Who Chooses His Path
---
Yūkan'na’s rise began with failure. Wounded in his first true battle, the young prince realised that birthright alone would never make him great. As he healed, three oaths were laid before him, each a different road to power.  
If he swore the Oath of the Shield, he would master defence, surviving every battle until he became a revered duelist and founded a Koryu of his own.  
If he chose the Oath of the Blade, he would rebuild himself through discipline, becoming a precise and deadly swordsman whose skill could decide any clash of steel.  
If he embraced the Oath of the Veil, he would turn from the sword entirely, delving into forgotten lore to become a subtle, formidable Shugenja blessed by his troops and feared by his enemies.  
Whichever oath he chose, Yūkan'na proved himself many times over. His name spread across the Jwar Isles sometimes as a master of defence, sometimes as a blademaster, sometimes as a commander wielding power that brushed the realm of the Kami. But all versions of his legend begin the same way: a humbled prince, standing at a crossroads, choosing the destiny that would define him.

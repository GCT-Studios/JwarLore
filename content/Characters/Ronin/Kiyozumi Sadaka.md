Sadaka was a young scion of the Ito Clan, and the Kiyozumi gained a powerful ally, and their political influence significantly increased with this marriage. For decades, Sadaka was the perfect samurai wife, looking after her husband's household and children, hosting guests and offering a channel to one of the great clans of the Jwar Isles. This all changed when her daughter was kidnapped. While [[Kiyozumi Haiboku|Haiboku]] immediately gave up and his mind broke, Sadaka showed not just her quality but her force of will and determination. She took control of the house and actually increased its holdings and fortune while she negotiated with two criminal cartels to look for her daughter. She personally led missions where information was found and when all else failed, she walked into the underworld and bent the vile creatures there to her will, swearing unspeakable oaths in return for her daughter's return. Perhaps, unfortunately, the bakemono managed to recover [[Kiyozumi Kichō|Kichō]] and return her to the Kiyozumi family. Kichō never recovered fully, and Sadaka was forced to uphold her side of that terrible bargain. While everyone was overjoyed to see the return of Kichō, it may have been better in the long run if she had never been found… 

Sadaka is now the head of the Kiyozumi family; even after his recovery, her husband simply isn't fit for such duties. She has agreed to allow her land to be used by both the Jung and the Blue Gulls in return for invaluable leads, which led her to her daughter. These organisations are working together in this region to smuggle contraband and eagerly agreed to renew the smuggling lanes through the town since [[Hozumi Daihanji|Daihanji]] had shut them all down decades ago.

Maha claims to be the daughter of a samurai family in a town far from her current situation. Maha made herself seem the perfect wife and mother; through her natural allure or some supernatural enchantment, [[Hozumi Daihanji|Daihanji]] married Maha, and Maha inserted herself into Hozumi's life. She certainly does not share the closeness of her new family to their people, often referring to them as her subjects and expecting the maximum show of difference every time she is seen outside. As the maternal figure for Koga, Maha has been entirely unsuitable. Indeed she only took an interest in the boy after [[Ryokeen]] appeared. In fact, even in his early day, Koga spent as much time away from his home as in it, often eating and sleeping in Taiyo's family house to be away from the unwelcome stepmother who dominates his family home. Much of the peasants' goodwill for the Hozumi family has been lost by Maha's presence, which has been heightened by several of the townspeople going missing entirely. Maha seems to be involved with other groups, and whether she owes them allegiance or their goals are temporarily aligned, it bodes ill for the towns and the Hozumi.

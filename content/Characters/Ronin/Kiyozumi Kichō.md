Youngest Daughter of the Kiyozumi family, Kichō is her [[Kiyozumi Haiboku|father]]'s favourite, and when she goes missing, his mind crumbles. Her family assumed her kidnappers would ask for ransom, some legal concessions or even use her as political leverage, but no demands came. In truth, Kichō was identified by the Shadow Crows of the Kage Kaze Zoku as having specific properties that would allow her to become one of the Soulless, who are unfeeling and emotionless killers who are all but undetectable and absolutely loyal to the Shadow Clan. Kichō was rescued from this fate, but much of the damage had already been done. She returned with no affection for her family, no youthful enthusiasm in her eyes and with her father's withdrawal into his depression, only her sister could get through to her. Whatever the ninja did to her, Kichō has been left knowing she should have an appreciation for her family, but the place where it should be is empty. Only her sister can coax emotional responses from her; Hinadori helps, allowing her to feel the love for her family and regain some humanity. But each time she feels herself slipping away, Kichō finds herself with tears on her face and no longer understands their meaning. It becomes harder and harder to remind Kichō of her true self, and as Hinadori wishes to spend more time with Koga, Kichō suffers her loss all the more. Koga has shown Hinadori a secret glade high in the mountains, which also seems to calm Kichō, and she visits it often. Perhaps some mystical creatures living among the peaks have also taken pity on Kichō?

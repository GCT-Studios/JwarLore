Haiboku inherited a flourishing house, wealthy beyond the dreams of most and with lands and influence to match; perhaps they will one day be a great house? While the Kiyozumi has consistently disregarded the peasants, and indeed all those of lower status, they certainly were secure enough in their position. Haiboku governed the family well for many years; he expanded their contacts among the great houses and even managed to marry a daughter of the Ito Clan. He was the very image of the daimyos of his family's past, expertly increasing all of his holdings. The very day [[Kiyozumi Kichō|Kichō]] vanished, Haiboku was broken. The realisation that he had done so many great things and made a formidable house, but he could not protect his favourite daughter, made his mind flee within himself. He was constantly second-guessing and dithering, reacting more and more slowly to events until he slipped into catatonia. He awoke to the sound of Kichō's voice. He rose and knew who he was and what had happened, but he would never fully recover. Haiboku knows he is not the leader he was and has left the governing of the house to his wife. The rumour is that he was physically injured trying to protect Kichō, and the family has allowed that rumour to stand, preferring that to the idea of admitting weakness. He still has some skill with blade and bow, which he can lend to the family. His family fortune can be wielded to encourage mercenaries to fight too. While Kiyozumi has studied the basics of battle strategy, this all goes out the window when his family is in danger. He will always risk everything to keep them safe; knowing he failed once has given him an obsession with personally ensuring all of his family are kept far from harm's way.

When Sadaka made her deal with the Smugglers, they sent her Kenmeina. While she acts as an ashigaru in service to the Kiyozumi, her function is to facilitate the smuggling operation and remove obstacles that threate the family or the deal they now have. Kenmeina's attitude is that she does a job. If you are paid to do a job, you do it properly. She is prepared to put her life on the line to get the job done and frequently has done so. While she is in service to the Kiyozumi, Kenmeina's first loyalty is to her smuggling group, and she knows that she has to remind the noble samurai of the deal they made whenever they get reluctant to see the work through. Wielding her heavy iron sword, Kenmeina is a competent, trained fighter and more than capable of holding her own, even against a samurai. Especially within the town where samurai rarely go armoured, Kenmeina is confident she could remove any opposition.

While the bargain the Kaidan made with Yueri in life was undoubtedly unnatural, dark and sinister, there is some hope for rebirth and resolution. Yueri has however corrupted this ray of redemption to its own ends. And continues in turn to mock not only the Kaidan but also those nearby turning success into failure at every turn and cursing their best endeavours.  
The Kaidan seems to haunts those near death, while ironically giving hope to New life. The twist being only evil yokai can be spawned, and the Kaidan is doomed to repeat its failures as the bargain keeps true death out of reach.

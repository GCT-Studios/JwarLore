---
title: Gyō Tochi
---
Gyō Tochi began his rise the Nezumi way, by being hurled into battle as expendable fodder. His masters expected him to die; instead, he skulked, hid, and struck only when backs were turned. Cowardice kept him alive, and survival earned him notice.  
Recognised for his uncanny ability to endure, Gyō Tochi was given command. He led with sharp orders rather than inspiration, always pushing others forward while keeping himself safely behind the swarm. His skills sharpened: stabbing from shadows, avoiding injury, and slipping through chaos untouched.  
As a Pack Master, he drove lesser rats with ruthless confidence. His inflated ego fuelled ever harsher tactics, yet beneath it lay genuine instinct. Gyō Tochi had survived more battles than most Nezumi live to see, guided by old, half‑forgotten ways of the warrens above and below. He believes he is intended for more. To prove it, he must one day face his destiny, a test he both fears and craves.  
For now, Gyō Tochi endures. And for a Nezumi, endurance is power.

The Kitsune are needed to fill many roles for Inari. Messengers, Tricksters, Judges, Scouts, Foxes...
Sometimes a younger fox needs guidance, an old soul, long separated from the mortal world will return to guide and teach a kit about their role. Masaru is unique in that she has two such fox-saints here to guide her. Atsukau seems to be teaching empathy, mercy and justice while Torikka is focussed on instilling fear in enemies and taking vengeance. Masaru must listen to both her advisors and use their lessons when best suited to please Inari.

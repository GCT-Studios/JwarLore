Long before she joined the Temple, Hiko had the spirit of the air within her, leading Hiko to explore far and wide, leaving her parents to worry. But that fear ended when she returned from exploring one day with a large mountain dog at her side that quickly became her stalwart companion. She named him [[Byiba]], and the two were inseparable even when the local monks laid claim to the massive dog who had once been theirs. But no matter how hard the monks tried, the dog stayed by her side. Not long after her interactions with the Temple, Hiko decided to join them, which was the only way the dog would return to his former companions.

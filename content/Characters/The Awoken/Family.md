While the other Jorogumo have little care for their followers, and few Kumo have any regard for those beneath them; it is the Kimura who display an uncommon love, admiration, and faith in those that they call their “Family”.  
Eager to share, help, and inspire those who need it, the Kimura refer to each other not by archaic titles as the Jaakuna do but by family names. They dote on those “Nieces” and “Nephews” who have joined the family later and respect their elder “Uncles” and “Aunts” who have raised them in the ways of the Kumo.  
Aided by their supernatural nature, they invite others to join them and despite their repulsive forms and brutish hulking nature, many join the Kimura willingly. They may join suddenly, even in the midst of battle or be courted over months. However they became a Kimura, they will not be afraid or shocked by their transformation. Any outsider seeing them grow new appendages, drink the vital fluids from captured victims, and express glee over their deformities and hideousness would only be more sickened.

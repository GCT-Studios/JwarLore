Much like the spiders they emulate, the Jaakuna men do not have a great life. Used for their blood, life force and manual labour, they adore and worship the more spider-like women even though none of them has ever progressed so far. All Jaakuna men are fated to die young, looking after crawlerling spiders ensures they receive many bites and lose a lot of blood before any enemies even see them.  
Touwa has managed to stay alive longer than others by procuring fresh food from trapped victims for his masters.

Like many of the Silvermoons street enforcers, Zhiv learned to fight on the streets. Only those who thrive there are invited to join the ranks of the Shatei. Zhiv has learned to overpower lesser fighters or those not accustomed to combat but against trained fighters, his inadequacies can become apparent. Luckily, Zhiv can take a punch as well as anyone and he really takes advantage when he gets an opponent to the ground.  
Like many of the newer Shatei, Zhiv asks the opponent if they can match his fighting style. Some may struggle to find the resources to Boost, or simply not have the option, in which case they fall to Zhiv's ability. But trained Monks or Samurai will easily counter his test and often return more than he can handle.  Using him against the correct enemy model is key.

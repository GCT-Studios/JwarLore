The crowd bayed for blood as the two Oni fought one another ferociously. Batsu standing firm, his thick legs planted in the sand of the Ring of Fire. His opponent BoBata was staying just out of reach of the mighty bell that he was swinging over his horned head. The tolling of the weapon was distracting BoBatu, the great clanging noise it fashioned as it swirled round and round and round, really set his nerves on edge. It reflected the light of the bonfires and torches as it made its thundering rotations. It was a weapon of beauty he thought, a weapon he desired for himself.

He circled his opponent slowly, carefully. He switched his spike-studded club from hand to hand to throw his opponent off balance. Keep him guessing he thought, keep him on edge. Batsu lunged forward releasing the bell at BoBato’s head. He saw it coming and ducked out of its path easily, he heard the chain slither and rattle past his ear as he moved out the way. Batsu was far cleverer than that though, he followed up the blatantly obvious attack with a swift kick to BoBata’s midriff, sending the younger of the two Oni flying. Dust rose into the air as BoBata came to a rolling halt. He was trying hard to catch his breath, that last kick had driven the wind from his lungs. His opponent was not waiting for him to get up; he was towering over him, the bell filling his vision. The noise was deafening.

He did his best to try and roll out of the way, but he was tiring quickly, and was moving far too slowly to escape. The bell connected one of his horns, sending a shockwave of pain through his skull. He groaned out loud. Batsu hearing this, pressed on with his attack even more ferociously. He kicked and stomped all over his foe’s muscular red body. But he too was tiring. They had been at this for hours. Two foes locked in a duel, a duel to the death.

BoBata managed to roll away this time. As the bell landed where his head would have been, he realised that he would not have survived such a powerful strike. He jumped to his feet and lashed out with his club, striking Batsu on the back of the head. The spikes penetrated the folds of skin with ease but ricocheted off his extremely tough skull. Blood sprayed from the deep puncture wounds landing on BoBata’s face. He licked the salty drops from his lips and smiled he loved the taste of it, he craved its iron texture on his tongue.

Batsu was fading fast now. His breathing laboured, his arms tiring, his knees starting to buckle. It was time for one last ditch attempt at victory. He dropped to his knees and grabbed a handful of sand. He spun around quickly and threw it into BoBata’s eyes, blinding his enemy. He threw himself into the attack. The bell tolled louder and louder as he struck BoBata with several brutal blows. His heart swelled with pride as he saw how much damage his adversary was enduring and yet, how he failed to yield to the furious onslaught.

BoBata was dimly aware of the audience howling for death and triumph. To die in combat was an honour. Death at the hands of an enemy was a glorious thing, but he was not going to die today. Today death beckoned for someone other than him. He went berserk, shrugging off numerous blows from his bell-wielding assailant. He closed quarters quickly, grabbing his enemy by the throat. He rained mighty blows down on his opponent’s face and head. He could feel his enemy start to weaken.

BoBata must end this quickly he realised. He did not have the strength to continue with this combat. He must kill his foe now, and end this bloody struggle. He struck his opponent over the eyes with his club, one of the spikes wedging deep inside the socket, lodging itself in Batsu’s skull. Batsu dropped to his knees, the fight leaving his once mighty frame. He was done for, he realised that now. He was going to die and yet, he had never felt happier, his training and administrations of his ward coming back to reward him, coming back to rip off his mortal coil.

The length of chain slid from his hands and the bell tumbled to the blood soaked sand as he collapsed on his back. He looked up at BoBata standing over him. He felt the young Oni looping the cold links of the chain around his massive neck. He felt the blood swell in his head, his eyes bulging from his sockets. All communication was cut off now, he could not speak, the chain tightened around his neck and he knew it was over. A paternal smile forming on his lips as the breath was squeezed from his lungs. He was dying! He was dying! He was...gone.

BoBata stood back from the body of the fallen Oni and howled into the night sky. His breathing ragged and laboured, his massive heart beating ferociously in his chest, he felt a wave of savage delight at besting his opponent. He was proud that, he, BoBata, had felled the mighty Batsu, no one else but him. He kneeled reverently before the body of his vanquished foe, watching the departed Oni’s soul waft its way upward, like a tendril of jet black smoke. He felt a sense of honour as it joined with his own. He perceived that his strength was far greater than before, the consumption of the dead Oni’s spirit, fuelling the potency and vigour that coursed through his mighty being. Even in death does an Oni live on. The souls of the fallen enemy, fuelling the vessels of the living...

He loosened the chain from around his dead father’s neck and raised high the bell. The throng of spectators chanted his name over and over again, as he swung it in a bloody arc around his head, spurring their shouts to even greater levels. “BoBata, BoBata, Bo-Ba-Ta!” yelled his brother Oni in admiration of his skill at arms.

” BoBata!” he roared, as he exited the ring, leaving his dead father behind, his body cooling in the dust.

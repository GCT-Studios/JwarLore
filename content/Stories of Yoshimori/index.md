## A City in Conflict

For a passing traveller Yoshimuri might look like every other city on the Jwar Isles.
Crowded with traders, crafters and peasants fulfilling their all-days’ work.  
But it has quite a rich history.  
It was once occupied by the Shiho Clan who exported the timber of the Twilight Wood
and the riches of the silver mine located at the southern outreach of the city. The Twilight
Wood was host to a big temple maintained by priests who served a deity long forgotten.
Many Shisei used to travel there for spiritual guidance. However, the Dragon War
changed everything. During the siege of Yoshimuri a great fire destroyed almost the
whole city including it’s temple. The priests were either killed by fire and sword or tried
to escape from the city.  

When the Takashi took over Jwar, Yoshimuri was rebuild. The river separated the city in
a northern and southern part. The North was given to the Minimoto to lumber the trees
and ship the wood down the river to Jyoto. The south is now managed by the Ito who
took over the silver mine and the rich rice fields. Someone could say neither of the two
clans are happy to share the city and so there are always fights, political struggles and
sometimes brawls in the city streets. Serving as arbiters in the city, with a small fort on a
river island, the Takashi don’t care as long as the taxes are paid and the store houses
are full.  

As if the situation wasn’t already boiling, recent incidents of unlucky events as sudden
disappearance of Clan envoys directly before important meetings, inexplicable vermin
plaguing the rice fields to weddings with tragic endings made life worse for the people of
Yoshimuri.  

While the people of Yoshimuri are busy with their daily problems, a spiritual imbalance
looms in the twilight wood, casting an occult shadow over them.

source: Stories of Yoshimori
---
While the Minimoto sought to expand their lumber industry by demolishing the old temple
and building a big sawmill to provide Jyoto and other Minimoto Provinces with enough
wood, the Ito saw a much bigger opportunity rising.
Not only could they secure a powerful Kami but also expand some territory at the
northern side of the Akegata River.
But how could they manage such strenuous effort with so less people.
So far from home the Ito in Yoshimuri were always short in numbers compared to the
Minimoto who could always rely on support from Jyoto.
Help came in an unexpected way.
The Minimoto were occupied with accidents at their lumberjack camps and major
setbacks to get their permission for the sawmill on the temple territory, important
requests went missing as if they have never been handed in.
The Ito seized this opportunity and managed to get one of their Shisei to the temple.
Under the watchful eye of the villagers, they tried to bind the Kami once again to its
sacred Honden.

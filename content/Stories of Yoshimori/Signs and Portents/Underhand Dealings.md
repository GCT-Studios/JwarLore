source: Stories of Yoshimori
---
Masaema Daisuke sat at a wooden
table in a corner of the Fan and Coin.
Usually, he would find some pleasure in
the gambling. Tonight, was different. He
had won some small amount of money,
but that had been spent on sake.
Regardless, he could still hear Aya’s
stern voice ringing in his ears.
She had gone to report back to Jyoto
and was not due to come back for
several weeks. This left him in charge
of the Minimoto garrison in Yoshimuri.
There were setbacks to his plans.
Especially after the Ito rebuilt the town’s
shrine. Things just kept getting worse.
Daily business now amounted to
struggles with the Ito shisai. Constant
bickering over the supposedly ‘holy’
trees which surrounded the temple. The
Minimoto were not permitted to harvest
the precious lumber. Not until the rituals
were performed, and of course, those
were expensive. When Daisuke wasn’t
arguing with the shisai, he was
placating uneasy woodcutters,
assuaging their fears of spirits and
yokai in the forests.
He stared at the bottom of his emptied
Sake cup and sighed deeply.
“You seem quite bothered my friend”.
Daisuke did not look up, he wasn´t
interested in Ikeda´s fake smile. Yet, he
was alone, and a sympathetic ear was
a better balm than most. Daisuke
voiced his daily struggles to Ikeda, and
the other man nodded in agreement at
the appropriate moments. After an
agreeable amount of time Ikeda spoke
up.
“I have heard that the Ito are stretched
my friend.” he said as he refilled
Daisuke´s cup. “They are far from Izu.
Have you seen their ashigaru? Those
men are ronin, who demand coin over
honour. They hold no loyalty to their
masters.”
Ikeda let the last comment hang in the
air as they both carefully sipped their
sake. Daisuke narrowed his eyes
thoughtfully and then Ikeda spoke
again.
“Their shisai only cross Minimoto
ground with guards, perhaps they will
be less confident if those guards find
their loyalty diverted.”
Ikeda swept his hand down to grasp the
sake bottle, an exaggerated gesture
that drew attention to the coin string tied
around his waist. It was heavy with a
good number of golden full moons. He
raised a quizzical eyebrow at Daisuke.
“That would be an excellent
development.” Daisuke said.
The two men raised their sake cups and
drained them dry.

source: Stories of Yoshimori

Akiko wiped away the sweat on her forehead. She had been sweeping floors of the rebuilt
temple the whole day. Annoyed by her chore, she overlooked a warped floorboard and
stumbled over it falling to her knees, knocking over the dirty water bucket.
Akiko hastily tried to collect the water with a towel back into the bucket, only to notice that it
drained between two floorboards.
Curiously Akiko knelt to check on it.
To her surprise the floorboard seemed a little bit loose. Prying the floorboard up she held her
breath. There was a small cache hidden beneath it.
With trembling hands, she reached in to reveal an old looking scroll case. She couldn’t help
but notice the Mon of the Shiho Clan on it. And there was still a scroll inside!
She unrolled the parchment and read the words inscribed upon it. Akiko always wondered
why there were three additional small Honden to the main temple. Now it perfectly made
sense. She needed to report this to Ito Kinu as fast as possible!
No more cleaning duty for this ambitious acolyte! she thought to herself.

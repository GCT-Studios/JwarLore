"Shoo! Go away! Don't ya defile our beloved shrine!" Roka said to a young woman, who
quickly left the market area in shame.
"What's this all about?"
a mature woman with a full basket of groceries stepped toward Roka.
"Didn't you hear? She sold her child to a demon."
"Oh my!"
"Yes she did! I couldn't believe it myself! Last year during the bleeding moon it happened."
Roka shook her head in disgust "Sana the fisherwoman saw her leave the baby basketed in
the Akegata river, only to be whisked away by a demonic shadow.” she hushed toward the
woman.
“If it weren't for Sana, nobody would have known! So if you see her preying on our sweet
Jizo statues, be sure to scare her away."

Dice rattled in a wooden cup. “Han!” “Chō!” “Chō” “Han!” the gamblers announced their bets loudly in
the smoke filled gaming hall of the Coin & the Fan.
A tense silence.
“Han!” the dealer announced, revealing the dice showing an uneven number of pips. Groaning and
exhilarating cries followed by the wooden betting-tablets clacking. .
Soruna collected her winnings, while a young man next to her, around 16 summers old, lost his last
betting tablet. Tears welled in his eyes. Was it from the smoke he wasn’t used to or because he was
cleaned out, she couldn’t tell.
“Here.” she offered him one of her betting tablets, almost instinctively.
“I don’t need your charity!” he stood up scornfully.
“Wait! You don’t understand! It is custom here.” she looked at him pleadingly “or I will suffer the same
fate as you!” He looked at her suspiciously. “Take it.” another woman urged “You’ll spare her and
yourself ill luck.”
“You are not from here, are you?” Soruna gave him an inquisitive look “Take it, just don't bet with it.
Cash it in immediately and stop gambling tonight” she pressed the wood into his hands and walked
away.
He looked at the weathered tablet inscribed with the house kanji. Another chance to get his money
back.
Dice rattled in a wooden cup. “Chō!” “Chō” “Han!” it echoed in the gambling hall.

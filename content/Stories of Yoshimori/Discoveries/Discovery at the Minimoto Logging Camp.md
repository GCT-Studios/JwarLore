„Do we really have to go further?” Haruichi’s whining voice came from behind.
Kazuto rolled his eyes.
“They say there are ghosts in the deep woods! Last week Himari and Naruki they
saw a ligh…-“
“Aww, come on! I will hear no more of that nonsense!” Kazuto interrupted. It was
already bad enough that they had to travel all the way through the dense underbrush
to cut trees but now he also had to endure all the complaining and stories about
ghost sightings. He kept hacking through a thick brush. Heavily sweating, he finally
made it through. But when he turned to see how Haruichi was doing, there was no
sign of him.
Instead, flickering lights caught his attention, little flames were dancing around the
branches of an old cedar tree, its cold blue fire seemed not to harm the branches or
the tree itself.
Kazuto frowned in shock, it couldn’t be real, could it? He tried to remember the
stories his grandmother told him about spirit flames but he was unable to focus. One
of the lights was moving slowly in his direction, so close he could see something shift
in it. It looked like a human face. His face!
He turned in panic and ran as fast as he could through the undergrowth, almost
stumbling over Haruichi. He looked at Kazuto's pale white face, eyes widened in
shock. He didn’t dare to ask and followed him in silence, back to the logging camp.

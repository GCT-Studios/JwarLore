Ito Takuya was very satisfied having such a profitable holding outside of the Izu
Islands. But it was hard to control, as supplying it from home took tiresome logistics
through neutral provinces. So to provide effective Ito governing, he not only provided
one but three leaders to Yoshimuri.
Ito Genkei oversaw the silver mining and commanded the local law enforcement. His
authoritative presence was only surpassed by his martial prowess. One never saw
him with a personal bodyguard, as he trusted his own abilities more than of others.
For the spiritual and diplomatic relations in the area Ito Kinu was present. A trained
Orochi shisai, who was as interested in converting people as she was in procuring
obscure knowledge.
Ito Mizuki’s use was to be politically betrothed to another samurai since she wed
Orochi instead. She was sent to Yoshimuri to watch the other two leaders and keep
them on their toes. Officially she is responsible for the fertile lands south of the
Akegata, but lacks the agricultural knowledge to be efficient.

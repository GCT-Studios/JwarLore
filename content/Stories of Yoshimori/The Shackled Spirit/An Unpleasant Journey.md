“I saw it with my own eyes!” the woodchopper said with a trembling voice.  

“A shadow lurking in the dark of the forest, I saw it, I swear!” he was all sweaty and heavy breathing.  

Aya resisted the urge to roll her eyes this was the third sighting of a so called “Shadow”. She had enough trouble since that mysterious fire destroyed the main sawmill. She was still looking for the person responsible for this incident. For sure it was this Ito scum.  

“It was just a wolf or something!” she answered, “I don’t-”  

“Enough!” Aya interrupted the woodchopper harshly. She really didn’t have time for this nonsense.  
“I will take care of it, now back to work!” with a wave of her hand she gestured him away. He bowed low and hesitantly hurried back towards the woods. She turned to one of her subordinates.  
“Send a patrol to the woods I want this wolves dead. We can’t afford that fear slows down the work!”  

“Yes, Ma’am and there is something else, new arrivals were spotted in the city heading directly towards the woods.”  

Aya sighed. She had a bad feeling about this.  

There was more trouble ahead of them.

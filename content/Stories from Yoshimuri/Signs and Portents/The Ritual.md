---
source: Stories from Yoshimuri
---
Ito Kinu took a deep breath. The ritual
strained her more than she had
expected. She could feel the anger of
the Kami swashing over her like a
crushing wave trying to exhaust her
mind and body. She needed to hold on
just a little longer.
She needed to seal the Kami back into
the shrine so that it will no longer
threaten the people of Yoshimuri. The
wooden wards were hanging from the
shrine roofs and started moving as if
touched by the wind.
Suddenly the inscribed kanji started to
glow forcing another wave of anger and
hatred down on Kinu meeting her
stubborn prayers and testing the
boundaries of the wards. There was no
way back for her, no room for failure.
She could not allow the Kami to break
free and strip itself from the shackles. In
the Ito Clan failure meant death.
Her prayers to Orochi grew louder. The
Skin on her arms turned scaly and for a
moment her eyes became reptiloid. She
pulled a knife out of her Kimono sleeve
and without any sign of pain she started
to carve a mysterious symbol into her
palm.
Blood dripped down on the stone floor.
The Kami gathered its last reserve of
strength to counter Kinu’s prayers in
hope to break free.
Silence filled the Twilight Forest.
Heavily breathing Kinu went down on a
knee, looking at the wooden wards. No
movement, no glowing lights. She
exhaled deeply and turned around.
They were still there.
In front of the shrine but in a few meters
distance the villagers of Yoshimuri were
gathering watching the ritual with
uncertainty and fear. But Kinu also saw
a spark of hope in their eyes. Hope of
living a peaceful life…finally.
“It’s done!” she proclaimed.
“There will be no more suffering for the
people of Yoshimuri!”
The villagers started to cheer and
bowed down in thankfulness. Although
she kept a polite smile there was
something more important: the Ito clan
finally had a holding on the other side of
the river.
And they would use and abuse it wisely.

source: Stories from Yoshimuri
---
Rokuhata's meditations were interrupted by thoughts of an almost floating shrine, so thin
were the stones that elevated it.. It was a place he felt familiar with. Was it an old memory
from when he was a child? Or was it something completely out of his fantasy? He couldn't
tell.
ΔΔΔ
His muscles tensed and ached from his day long katas at the monastery. His teachers
reprimanded him often for being unfocused lately. It felt more like a chore without any
improvement. All because the vision of this mountain shrine interrupted almost every
thought. It just wouldn't cease.
Not being able to improve his studies he felt out of place and restless. He couldn't continue
like this. He had to search for this place.
ΔΔΔ
Leaving the temple life behind, he found himself traversing the snowy iron mountains. Where
to exactly, he didn't know, but the temple provided him with warm farewells, clothes,
provisions and skill to survive this harsh environment.
Will it be enough until he found his visions destination? Did it even exist?
First he tried to search the mountains by instinct alone. This didn't lead him to anything
substantial.
Then he tried to concentrate on it, trying to will it into existence.
But nothing happened.
Weeks have gone by without any contact to any living being.
He thought of building it, but didn't know how to start.
His provisions faded, the cold biting even harder, he still didn't abandon his will to find this
holy place. He couldn't be wrong, could he? Exhausted, he fell asleep.
ΔΔΔ
He dreamt of the shrine once again. A warm and green oasis in the icy tundra. Twin
waterfalls sprang from the temple stairs into the rocks below. Little bonsai trees grazed the
elevated area, blooming as if it were spring. Something changed though.
From his elevated area, he saw to the horizon, plains and forests before him. He even
spotted a small town at the river.
Was he still dreaming this tranquil dream or was he really there?
Was this peace he was feeling?

Kneeling beside the rice field, Chō shook her head in disbelief. She turned to Himari
who stood next to her.
“Another crop lost to the “plaque”. Chō sighed.
The tried-out rice fields' soil had changed to a reddish color and weird fungi had
started to devour the small rice plants.
“That’s not all, I found some weird tracks in one of the tainted fields.”
Chō transfixed her with a questioning look.
“They looked like animal tracks but they were human sized, with three toes!” She
kept talking while Chō stood up and dusted her hands off.
“I don’t know any animal with this kind of footprints.”
“Me neither.” Chō mumbled “Did you report this to the guards?” She pointed at the
field in front of them
Himari huffed angrily “Like they are of any use. Goku was the last one who reported
such oddities, they were just laughing and calling him a fool not able to maintain a
field properly. Although everyone knows that the Ito are complete amateurs when it
comes to farming.” The last part she was almost whispering so only Himari could
hear her.
Both burst out in laughter.
Chō’s face straightened. “But seriously we need to do something. Gather the others
at the hour of the rat. We must figure something out on our own.”
Himari nodded in agreement. She took one last look at the field then she went back
to work.

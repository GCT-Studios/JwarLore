source: Stories from Yoshimuri

“There, etched into the dirt, were strange tracks unlike any we had ever seen!
At first, we tried to rationalize it, thinking it could have been some sort of animal. But as we followed
them deeper into the mine, we felt it was something far more sinister.”
“The further we went, the more the air grew thick with an eerie silence. It was as if something was
watching us, waiting for us. Preying on us. And then, in the shadows we saw IT!”
“A massive, hulking creature with a horned head and glowing red eyes emerged from the dark. It let
out a deafening roar, causing us all to scatter through the tunnels and run for our lives!”
The Miners sat around a campfire listening in excitement, all eyes on Matsuhige.
He enjoyed the attention. They were soaking in every word like sponges.
He made a prominent pause.
Then he burst into laughter. A moaning went through the crowd. Some were angry and ashamed,
some joined into the laughter.
“You should have seen your faces”. He laughed at them, mimicking and exaggerating the frightened
faces of his listeners.
“You are such a fool!” one of the men stood up and pushed him in anger.
The crowd scattered. Some went to their tents, some went back to work. The miners had to work in
shifts.
Matsuhige grabbed his pick axe and lantern to follow the other workers back into the mine. His lantern
casting long shadows on the wall and ground. Still giggling he passed some of the other workers while
he had to move deeper into the mine.
After a while his work was interrupted by an odd sound coming from a side path.
“Hello?” He lifted the lantern up to have a better look into the tunnel.
There was a big crack in the wall of the tunnel, almost wide enough to squeeze through it.
He raised his lantern again. “Guys? Are you trying to prank me? He asked with a trembling voice.
Two red eyes were glowing in the dark of the rift piercing him with an evil stare.
A foul wind gushed through the crack, ripping the lantern out of Matsuhige’s hand and extinguishing
the light.
The last thing that Matsuhige heard was a growl that let his body shake like an earthquake.

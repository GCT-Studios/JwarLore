“Do! Kote-Men!” the cries of the ashigaru blasted through the training yard. Uto sensei walked among
them, exposing faults and correcting mistakes with a Jo, sometimes even with a jab to an opening in
their defense.
“This wouldn’t have been enough in the Dragon War! You would have all perished as the Shiho claws
swooped past your spears and gutted you!” they goaded the ashigaru.
“Do! Kote-Men” the soldiers spurred on their group kata, sweat pouring inside their armour.
“Don’t be so harsh on them, Uto-sensei.” Takashi Noburo waved off the midday heat with a fan “They
have a long journey ahead. You should go easy on them”. Uto sensei walked over to the man on the
dojo railing. Wearing a beige hakama and fine blue kimono with golden dragons embroidered on his
sleeves. The hair worn in a fashion meant more for courtly appearings than daily business.
“It’s the only lesson I teach, given the limited time I had with them. Only because some common
caravans have gone missing over the Ataka Plains, we’re sending off my semi-trained troops!” they
replied, anger in their voice.
“You are being too harsh on your skills as a teacher. You were my sensei as well and I turned out
fine”.
“Yes.” the mumbling reply came. “We can’t spare any of them. After the decision of letting Ito pass
through Minimoto territory. Minimoto only respects strength. There is trouble ahead, I can feel it in my
bones.”
“Are you doubting my decision again!” the fan clacked close.
“No, Noburan-san. Your decision has been made and as samurai I will abide. My concern is merely
for the future of Yoshimuri. Our grip on this town is loosening. We need these men to keep our
control-”
“Enough! I will have no more of your ramblings. See to it that the men are rested and well prepared.”
“Hai. Owari!” sensei Uto bellowed disgruntledly.

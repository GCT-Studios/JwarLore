source: Stories from Yoshimuri
---
Not long after the first heavy drops of rain
splashed down on the dried ground
Thunder was rumbling in the distance.
Asahi searched for cover under a big cedar
tree. She always enjoyed the smell of rain,
especially in the woods where it was mixed
with the smell of wet timber.
Seeing movement by the ruins, she first
thought her friend finally joined her, but as
more brushwood bustled, she was careful
to not draw any attention.
Strangers emerged out of the bushes; they
were gathering at the clearing where the
once great temple now lied in ruins.
She tried to hide behind the cedar trees big
roots so the others couldn’t see her.
Although she only heard indistinct chatter,
she could at least watch the scene unfold.
They were building a circle, while one of
them took out some slips of paper.
Something was written on them, it reminded
her of the seals, Master Eito used to
decorate the town shrine with. He once
explained it would keep bad spirits away.
The stranger spread them all over the ruins
and fixed them with smaller stones.
Then he joined the others in the circle.
They started chanting in a language that
seemed familiar, but she couldn’t
understand anything.

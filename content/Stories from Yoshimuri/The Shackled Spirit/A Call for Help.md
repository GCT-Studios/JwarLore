source: Stories from Yoshimuri
---
Not again Ikeda thought to himself, when
he heard that familiar sound of a brawl
coming from the street. The conflicts
between Minimoto and Ito became now
more recently and it started to worry him.
His establishment was of an exquisite kind,
that kind people would go for different sorts
of entertainment. His guests were not only
wealthy people from the town but also
travellers and rich merchants who heard of
The Fan and The Coin’s reputation of
gambling and sensual pleasures.
But all these fights recently were bad for
business.
There was silence for a moment followed
by some noisy chatter on the streets. He
kneeled down and shoved one of the shoji
paper screens aside just as much as he
could peek out on the street. Something
was strange, the people on the streets were
not the usual sorts. He could see a person
lying on the ground. Another kneeling
beside it calling for help. Ikeda scratched
his chin.
He knew interference often lead to trouble
but he also knew that desperate people
were often eager to pay every price to get
what they needed. He looked again at the
strangers on the streets. Yes these ones
looked very desperate. He chuckled and
stepped out of the house.

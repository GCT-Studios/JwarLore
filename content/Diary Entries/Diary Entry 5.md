Tendrils of fog snake around my legs, an iron bell tolls far in the distance. Darkness has fallen on the land and I am at peace. I walk, I think, I take in the air. I feel the vibrations of the city through the soles of my feet. I smell food being cooked for supper. I am here, walking among them, observing them in their nightly rituals, but I am not part of it. I was, many years ago, but no longer. My rituals are much darker now.

I make my way to the outskirts of the city to where they bury the dead. The odour of decay is powerful here. This is where my love now resides.

Under a stunted cherry tree is her grave. It has been disturbed by scavengers. I see her skull partially covered by twigs and fallen leaves. Even in death I can see the face of the one that I love. I should be awash with emotion, my heart filling with sadness...

But I feel nothing.

Or do I? I don’t know anymore, I am confused. My work is the only area in which I have complete control. It is in the other arenas of my existence that I struggle to comprehend what is going on. Things are muddled.

I have no sense of loss or longing, just a dark hole that should be filled with _something_... Should it be anger maybe? Anger because my love was taken too young?

Rage perhaps? Rage in that we were newlyweds when they came and dragged us from our beds in the middle of the night?

Something akin to horror? The horror I felt when I watched them run her through with their swords? Over... and over again... the steel, like blood-stained bones, sticking out of her chest, her back, her neck, the blood pooling around her on the ground. I pick up her skull and stare deeply into the hollow sockets seeking something, anything.

Alas, I knew her, my poor love, a woman of infinite beauty. Now those lips that I kissed so often are but a mockery of what they once were. The cheek that I once stroked now worm food, her slender neck gone, the heart I longed to fill, cold and rotten and lying somewhere in the ground.

I reach for the masks I carry with me everywhere. If I could but place one on her and watch her return to me as she was. But it will not work, she is too far gone, the corruption of her flesh is now complete. It would require a power far blacker than mine to right this wrong. My beloved Ikiryo, how I loved you!

I gaze at her skull in my right hand and the mask in my left. Oh , if it were just so simple to place one on top of the other and let the dark gift work its power! But no, it is not meant to be. I would do anything to bring her to me from beyond the pale. To walk with me on the path toward glory, the sacred trail of death and destruction I aim to bring to these isles, these pathetic islands of Jwar. They have taken from me and I will pay them back in return. My wrath shall be magnificent to behold when I unleash the power of the Cult upon them.

I hang my head, but no tears come to me. Some days I just feel so awfully tired, so very alone, I feel the enormity of my task pushing down on me, trying to force me into the earth and let it consume me. And if that did indeed happen? If the isles opened its maw and swallowed me whole? What would happen then? Well it would spit me out in great haste, because I am a bad seed. I aim to poison them from within. I kiss the skull gently and put it back on the ground. I hear the distant rumble of thunder out over the ocean. Lightning licks the rain clouds. I stand up and walk home. There is a storm coming, and that storm is me, Kato Kubiashi shall be the ruin of you all...

I seek the ones you have cast out, those who failed to meet your grand expectations, and the few who dared to challenge your insipid laws. These are the people I draw toward me, living or dead, it makes no difference to me. I see no distinction between the two.

In life they were worthless to you, but now in death I shall raise them up. I shall turn your very sons and daughters against you. The bell tolls for you all; together my Kairai, we can change the order of things. We can ravage and burn and take back what is rightfully ours. We can destroy the light and bring darkness to the lands. We suffer not under the boot heel of oppression.

The more you execute, the more powerful my army becomes. Fear the legions of the mask whelps, for it is coming, oh yes, it is coming. As sure as the snows follow the sun, we are on the move. I take what you have so callously thrown away and disposed of in your high minded cleansings, and I make it mine... mine to use as I see fit. I give the dead a purpose again! I want to bring wrack and ruin, and spread fear amongst you all. I spit in your empty graves!

I smell the rot as they march; I stare at the simplicity of their burial shrouds. I delight in the bone that breaks the withered flesh. All of this decay excites me beyond description. And when I see the sunken face put on the mask for the first time, I become exalted. The masks, how beautiful they are, a simple white, like the pale underbelly of a gutted fish, oh put it on brothers and sisters, put on the masks and rise! These are not bodies that you bury, but the seeds of soldiers to be used against you in the War that is to come. Corpses? You call them corpses? Fool! These are the harbingers of your doom, your destruction and your death. And death? Do not fear it. It comes to us all sooner or later, some sooner, rather than later.

But who is it who lives behind my eyes? I fear the presence of another in my thoughts. Something is slipping. I stare at my visage in the water and it speaks not the truth. Inside I am dead and cold to this world but my reflection tells another tale. It is a horrid and gambolling thing, a rictus grin that chills my marrow and leads me to believe that there must be another inside. One as subtle as the opium when it first comes on. One that tries to control the Master? It cannot be! I dance to one tune and one tune only, and that is mine! I am no one’s puppet...Save your sympathy! I need it not dogs...

I feel an uncertain energy thrumming through my limbs. It makes me light headed. My stomach churns with nausea. My head aches. The pressure is building steadily behind my eyes. It is taking all my strength to write this down. I must sharpen my will to continue my work; nothing stands in the way of greatness. Not life, not death, no hesitation can crowd my mind.

I will harness my strength over will and continue with my purpose. Nothing is more important to me than to see it wash over this world, a world that I shall build on the bones of others. Corpulent and corrupt, they are puss-filled maggots that I shall expose to the light. I shall grind them down as the miller grinds the wheat, and when all that is living is dead and their citadels burn, who will then oppose us?

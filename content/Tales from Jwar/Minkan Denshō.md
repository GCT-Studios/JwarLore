The enemy fled, the ground shook, but rather than the fear of their charge that we suffered a few scant minutes ago, the figure felt relief as they would not need to be fought again for some time.  

Even with his head bowed and his hat covering his eyes, the lone figure’s ever-squinting eyes noticed a peasant emerge from the brush.  
“You fought them off!”  

The figure did not move, he continued to clean his weapons and put them back in their many sheaths.  

“And those with you. I never thought they were real!”  

In his mind he wanted to snark back asking which she didn’t believe in, monkeys or gorillas but to be honest Ashinaga Tenaga had come from the lake to help. His Grandmother told him those tales but he never believed. All kinds of mischief was blamed on the Kitsune but in the city, even the stories were few and nobody thought they were real. Come to think of it, fighting alongside wild monkeys and that baboon is not a normal thing to happen.  

The villager noticed his Daisho and knelt before him. “I’m sorry my lord!  I did not know you.”.

“Get up. I’m nobody’s Lord”.

She stayed prostrate before him.
“My Lord, will those creatures you fought alongside be back? I thought they were creatures of myth and dream?”  

“Dream?  Maybe that's it. Maybe I am dreaming?”  
The Samurai didn’t know how he ended up in this battle or why the folklore creatures followed him into battle.  

“No dream, my Lord! I saw you kill 20 of them!”  

“Yeah…” he replied softly, voice full of regret, “Sounds like one of my dreams.”.  
His voice came from further away and Atsuko looked up, but the samurai was nowhere to be seen.

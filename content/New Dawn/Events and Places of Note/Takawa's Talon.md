These narrow, sheer cliffs rise a colossal one and a half leagues into the sky, curving outwards to three sharp points, like the claw of a bird, from the angry waters below. These choppy waters are told to be the last resting place of the Tengu. Individuals make their way here, far from their homes in the Iron Mountains, when they can no longer use their wings. This, their last pilgrimage takes them to the very peak of the Talon. Here it is believed they meditate on the life they have lived before they take the last flight of the Tengu.

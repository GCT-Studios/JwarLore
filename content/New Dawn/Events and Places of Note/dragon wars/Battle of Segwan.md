The first battle of the Dragon Wars. A small force of the Minamoto clan were defeated by a
combined force of the Horse Lords of Ataka and the Shiho clan, just north of Ataka. The Minamoto were led by the eldest Takashi son; Takashi Akira. Whilst it was only a skirmish during the opening gambits of the war, Akira was killed. The Takashi clan know the vital importance of maintaining an alliance with the Minamoto clan but secretly hold them responsible for his death.

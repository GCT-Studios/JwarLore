These brother and sister islands are most famous for the wide ranging species of tropical birds that inhabit them. Many weird and wonderful animals dwell here and in recent years the Silvermoon gumis have made handsome profits trapping and shipping these exotic wonders out of the Jwar Isles to populate the gardens of many wealthy clans. The more dangerous specimens are often kept and trained for the fighting pits of Jima, further lining the pockets of the Syndicate.

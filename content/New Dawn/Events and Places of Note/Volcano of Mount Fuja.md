The giant pools of lava found in this the most active of volcanic craters across the Jwar Isles are constantly bubbling and shifting. Located in the mountain ranges of ‘Koun no Yama’ just north of Jima, the chaotic and destructive nature of the lakes draws the monks of Ro-Kan and allows them to truly reflect upon the element of Fire. The magnificent strength of nature on display here is a wondrous sight and breathes life into the Kami of fire.  

Visiting this site in recent years has brought the monks into conflict with the more nefarious elements of the Silvermoon’s trading operations, the alleged smuggling of goods through the mountains but more importantly the Silvermoon’s treatment of animals invoking anger in even the most placid of monks.

The location of the cove remains a mystery to the authorities of the Jwar Isles. The only information they have been able to gleam is from ransomed lords whose wild yet recurring claims describe a secluded cove, capable of housing an army of ten thousand in deep caves cut into to the cliff face, and shelter for an armada that would be unrivalled by the major clans combined. How this fortress remains a secret is known only to the Jung pirates.

A unique set of conditions exist that created this giant atoll. Steady sea temperatures and rich nutrients leached from The Plain of Tears have allowed this huge organism to thrive. This mass of coral lies mostly just below the surface, where it does breach the surface of the water the giant Jwarian tortoise thrives.  

This atoll has proved the ruin of many merchant vessels over the years, effectively sealing off the coastline from Ugo to Itsiro’s Peak, its waters deadly to all but those that know it best, the local fishermen. Whilst still risky they ply the waters in their shallow bottomed boats to take advantage of the abundant sea life that make Onikitori Atoll their home. Rumours abound of secret waterways through the atoll known only to the Jung pirates that once called Ugo their home.

The gulf is crisscrossed with merchants carrying goods between Eddo, Ryu, Okyo and Jima. Its naming remains contentious amongst scholars. Some argue the isles of Jwar, Izu and Wakasu were once, in the times of creation three petulant brothers turned to stone by Kami who could no longer look upon and listen to their bickering.  
Others claim that the name merely came from the prominence of the trade between Eddo, Ryu and Okyo. This is scoffed at by the former, making mock suggestions that with the emergence of Jima it should be called the ‘Gulf of the Four Brothers’.

A frigid land mass, the most northerly island of the Jwar Isles has little farm land and the temperatures rarely venture above freezing. Its mountains are home to the white yak, the hardiest of animals, and these provide much needed sustenance for the small Yoshido clan along with warmth from their thick hides. The trees that grow here are also known for the robust and flexible wood they provide and would be in great demand were it not for the costs based on the difficulties of transporting the cargo to market.

Fukata was once the richest merchant in the Jwar Isles, his wealth was becoming of some renown amongst even the most powerful clan leaders. His name now though is only linked to making a foolish error. During the reign of the second Emperor, Fukata successfully grew his imports and exports business from a market stand in the docks of Okyo into a hugely successful business, owning as many as a ruling clan and warehouses across the major cities of the Jwar Isles. For all his trading acumen he was never able to shake his status as a merchant and achieve the political recognition he deserved, leading him down a path to ruin.  

He vied to buy the power he coveted, enlisting minor clans and mercenaries at every opportunity, his wealth allowed him to amass a vast host. He did though underestimate his opponents and the ‘power’ of the establishment. While Fukata offered the chance of wealth and plunder if the battle was won, the clans offered legitimate titles and the associated wealth if Fukata were to lose. On the
morn of battle as a light mist dispersed so had Fukata’s assembled army.  

He was used as an example against opposing the power of the clans. Today in the Jwar Isles people making foolish decisions or ambitiously aiming too high are often mockingly called Fukata in memory of his folly.

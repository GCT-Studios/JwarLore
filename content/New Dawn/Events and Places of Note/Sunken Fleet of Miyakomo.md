Many years ago when the Empire was in its infancy the Daimyos of Jwar refused to bow the knee to the Emperor. The Empire raised an impressive fleet to crush any resistance from the far flung Jwar Isles. This was challenged with all the might of the seafaring clans of these island peoples. In the Chrome Sea they met and a fearsome battle ensued, the local warlords knew their seas and
inlets well but the sheer weight of numbers bearing down on them proved too much. Their forces were scattered and their most powerful leader Miyakomo decided to trust his fate to the seas rather than be taken by his enemy; this young upstart from the mainland. Into the water he
was followed by his men. To the man they drowned, never to be seen again. Local legends tell that they were saved by the ghosts of their ancestors, forced into a hybrid form with the crabs previously fished from these parts. There are prophecies that tell they will return to exact revenge
on the lesser clans of the Jwar Isles that left them to their fate and even prospered in their absence under imperial rule. While many believe this to be old unsubstantiated tales, the crabs with the faces of samurai are still returned hastily to the sea.  

Those that ply their trade in these waters avoid this site and those that have ventured closer have all kinds of wonderful claims of men with pincers and the shell of a crab for a back.  

From Itsiro’s peak one can still see the remnants of this great battle, a time and sea weathered mast juts defiantly from the salty water here and there.

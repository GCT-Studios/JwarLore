It is midnight. Outside, the isles are in darkness and the moon rides high. It is colder here in the mountains than I remember. A fire burns brightly in the grate taking the edge off the chill that surrounds me. But I fear that no amount of fire will ever warm these bones of mine again. To prove this I run my fingers through the candle’s flame. My skin grows red and burns. I like the sensation, I need it. It makes me feel connected to the world that surrounds me. The quill moves over the pages of my diary while my other hand roasts. The words flow out across the paper. I have been up for many days now, studying the lore of the Prefecture, the philosophies of the monks, the myths of the Oni. Slowly and steadily, I draw my plans against them.

I seek to understand what I want to overthrow. By studying the habits of the enemy I expose them for the charlatans that they really are. They are weak, while we are strong. I seek not reward for I what I do, no, not at all. I seek revenge.

I pray to all that is dark and desperate and shunned by the followers of good. The Monks speak of balance and order; I would see it collapse around their praying ears. It must become chaos and disorder, a maelstrom of anarchy unleashed upon the world. Nature in itself appears to be ordered but if you look beneath the surface, it is but scarlet in tooth and claw. Only the strong can be allowed to survive. Not the weak, nor the foolish, none other than the Cult of Yurei shall inherit.

The Prefecture holds their ideals high, as high as their War banners. We would watch those standards fall to the ground, the colours run into the mud and blood of battle. Their marshalled lines of soldiers break and flee when they realise just what the Cult stands for, what we are _made_ of... How shall their faces look, when they recognise their foe as ones that were once friends, family even? It brings a smile to my face, I cherish the thought.

What of those magnificent beings that dwell in our psyche? What of them? The Oni they are called. They offer a glimpse into a history we have come to forget. And forget we do, all too often, but none shall ever forget the Cult. This I swear. As I sit here at my desk, writing, watching the logs splutter and burn, a sense of purpose steals over me.

But wait! There is a knocking, as if someone is gently rapping upon the cabin’s door. Maybe it is the wind in the trees that plays tricks on me. But no, there it is again. How droll, I have a visitor. Who could it be at such a late hour so far from civilisation? I don’t know, nor do I think I want to. But the knocking is louder this time; I must see who it is.

Oh let the darkness be praised! She has come back to me! My beloved Ikiryo is mine once more. I open the door and at first see nothing but the night. I peer deep into the darkness thinking the knocking had been an illusion when the silence is broken. I hear a woman say my name. Softly at first, I hear it as one hears the spider that spins her silken web. But I hear it in my mind rather than on the breeze. Then it comes again, louder this time, and then, by the Cult, she walks out of the darkness and into my arms. She sits here beside me now as I write this. The mark of the Cult is heavy on her; the eyes that I loved to stare into for hours will forever be open. And her lips have been made even prettier by the charnel house cotton that binds them together. They will be mine to kiss once more.

I will never doubt the power of the Cult, for here beside me is _living_ proof. It was but mere months ago that I held her skull and bemoaned her demise! And yet here she is, or is _she_? Is it really her? I don’t know, and yet I dare not question, I must merely rejoice. She looks at me out of the corner of her eyes while I write. She watches me and waits, watches and waits... what does this all mean? I thought her eyes were green, but these look more hazel in the fire, maybe I am confused. It is perhaps too much for me to take in, my mind must be overwrought.

Her countenance is _grave_, her decorum stern. She is staring into the fire now; it appears as if she is looking into the void. I believe that if she reached out her arms she could touch it. Her skin so pallid; I can imagine those cold hands stroking someone’s cheek and draining the very life from their bones. A sense of power resonates from my Ikiryo; it sits inside her like a coiled snake ready to strike. I look up, and she is watching me again. I feel a shiver go down my spine and I don’t think it’s the cold mountain air anymore.

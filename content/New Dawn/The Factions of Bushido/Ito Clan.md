The Ito clan’s mon was that of the snake, long before its occupation of Izu and its members took up the worship of Orochi. The nature of its members however, has always inherently reflected their mon and has forever been questioned by that of the other major clans. The Ito also have a look that reminds some of the snake; thin almost gaunt physiques and darting eyes always seeming to weigh the situation, always looking for an advantage. The Ito have never been accused of loyalty and thus they have never risen as high as their ambitions. They were both shocked and bitterly disappointed when the Shiho clan bestowed stewardship of Izu upon them; the island’s capital Okyo, whilst defendable, is the home of countless immigrants and paupers.  

It was on the island of Izu though that the Ito truly became the Snake clan. The Ito have never been considered devout followers of any belief system, but like the other major clans they adhered in their own way to Michi. On their arrival they were bemused by the range of beliefs
and religions on Okyo. However, because of their affiliation with the snake, the small and sorry looking Temple of Orochi, an ancient snake Kami, caught their interest. What was to follow surprised them all. It began with the old and tired priestess Naoko, who told wild and fanciful prophecies about the return of Orochi and the rise of the snake. The Ito's pride led them to the assumption that the snake referred to their clan and supported Ito Itsunagi in a far reaching and complicated mission to fulfil the prophecies and their destiny.  

The awakening of Orochi has bestowed a new found purpose upon the Ito clan and its blessing has given them the strength to take what they believe is rightfully theirs.  

Taking the blessing of Orochi through the bite of a snake, can lead to a transformation reflecting in some way the visage of Orochi or a slow and lingering death for those deemed unworthy of Orochi’s blood. To prove the strength of Orochi, Naoko was the first to receive the venom of Orochi and she has also moved furthest along the path of transformation. It is unknown where this will take her or anyone else as each individual that survives the ritual has so far expressed Orochi in many varied and different ways. Whilst any can accept the blessing, only women can become Shisai of Orochi, these priestesses, of sorts, are fanatical in their devotion to Orochi and there are
some within the Ito family that wonder if a decision had to be made, where their true loyalties would lie. The same reluctant voices are also uneasy about the manner in which the acolytes of Orochi are so easily and pervasively being accepted into the upper echelons of the clan. Behind closed doors questions are being asked as to the final price the Ito family will pay for their new found power.  

Initially Orochi’s followers attempted to hide away the effects of the transformation, but as their strength and numbers grew they revealed themselves to the people of Izu. This created great dissent amongst the common man, they felt it was an affront to the Kami, that the blessing of Orochi produces abominations. The Ito’s devotion to Orochi, a Kami that does not accept worship of any other, is too far removed from the balanced state in which the religions of Okyo had coexisted in relative peace for generations.  
Many resistance groups formed, their goal to remove the Snake clan from power. They united to form the IRF – the ‘Izu Resistance Front’ and for a time their numbers grew and they became a serious threat to the Ito’s rule. The IRF’s increasing size and organisation were also its weakness
and the Ito were quick to act. They began successfully infiltrating the increasingly confident IRF and what followed will forever be known as the ‘Night of a Thousand Daggers’. The Ito, desperate to put down this rising threat, hired the Shadow Wind clan to eliminate the command group of the IRF in a single night. The leaders of the IRF were slain while they slept and although only three
hundred or so were killed the Ito’s propaganda machine quickly created panic amongst the people. In the weeks that followed rumours circulated of a master list in the Ito’s possession detailing all members of the IRF. Support evaporated and the remaining members fled the city. They have not given up however and they take inspiration from the likes of the Shiho clan. Ito spies are also investigating the possibility of support from the Shiho to the IRF.  

The events that followed the ‘Night of a Thousand Daggers’ were then followed by the Ito’s declaration of its withdrawal from the Prefecture of Ryu and the assertion of its own divine right to rule the isles and beyond. This was an uncharacteristically bold move for the Ito clan, who in recent history have favoured less direct means to achieve its goals. It has also been openly bolstering its own support, through the creation of the Jaguar clan. A signal of their intention to see their plans play out to the bitter end. The support of the Mori family and their ascension to clan status was vitally important to the Ito, adding more than simple numbers, it has shored the defence of Izu by securing the landing beaches to the west of the island. The timing of their announcement could not have come at a worse time for the Prefecture of Ryu. The leaders of the Prefecture; the Dragon clan led by the Takashi family are fighting on all fronts and need to be able to rely on the support of the clans. Even with the Ito clan's new found military confidence it can still only count upon a relatively small force of regular ashigaru. It has however a secondary fighting force attached to the Temple of Orochi. A force able to form fully battle ready warriors, who excel in the support of the clan’s main army through stealth, speed and subterfuge.  

The prophecies are being fulfilled, an old power is rising and the Ito are ready to cross the Silver Strait and finally take what should always have been theirs; rule of the Jwar Isles and beyond.

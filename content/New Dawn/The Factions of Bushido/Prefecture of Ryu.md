The Prefecture of Ryu is the furthest lying province on the eastern edge of the Empire. One of seventy two Prefectures that make up the Empire of Golden Sun, it consists of a series of islands known collectively as the Jwar Isles. The Prefecture of Ryu has had many rulers during its existence, the current being, quite fittingly, the Dragon clan led by the Takashi family, but this has not always been the case and rule of the Prefecture has changed many times over the years since the first Emperor; Takawa Shiro (Reigned 0–49 SK).  

The Prefecture of Ryu like many of the Empire’s fringe provinces is left to rule itself quite autonomously, provided the taxes continue to flow westward. The Empire rarely intervenes in internal disputes, only doing so if conflicts last long enough to slow the steady flow of moons back to the capital’s coffers. The Prefecture of Ryu is also expected to supply troops to the Empire when called upon. The six major clans recognised in the Prefecture are all required to swear allegiance to the Prefecture’s ruling clan and thus the Empire.  

Rulers of a Prefecture that face rebellion have to weigh up the benefit of support from the Empire’s military might in quashing a challenge to their rule, with the loss of honour and status at court implied by not being able to manage one’s own affairs. The Dragon clan have at present decided to deal with the Snake clan on their own, for now. Clans that take the decision to rise against the rulers of a Prefecture never take the decision lightly, any clan unwilling to be part of the Prefecture are considered enemies of the Empire and thus need to resolve any conflict quickly and decisively ready to prove they are more suitable to lead. Changes in leadership of a Prefecture are rare, increasingly so as you move closer to the Empire’s capital, such is the stability around these regions. That said, these are troubled times in the Jwar Isles, as the further Prefectures have seen their fair share of ambitious families looking to carve out a piece of the Empire as their own.  

The Takashi clan have taken control of a Prefecture during the most difficult of times. The Dragon Wars took their toll heavily on most clans across the Jwar Isles but the Dragon led the assault on the Eagle’s rule and took the most dramatic losses during this time, bringing them back in line with their supporters. A seemingly ever increasing list of challenges are mounting against the Dragon. The other clans have the scent of blood in their nostrils and stand poised to take advantage at the slightest sign of weakness, the Dragon is fighting to hold the clans together; losing the Ito from the fold is worrying but it is perhaps better than facing the Bear from the north. On top of this, Shiho raids across the Isle of Jwar, have caused a headache Takashi Hida, the leader of the Dragon, could do without. The Shiho continue to target merchants along the Iron Road distributing some of the spoils amongst the people, gaining the rebels wealth and support from the common man. Hida, an astute leader, also understands the importance of trade and commerce to his rule, protecting them fiercely. By increasing Prefecture patrols he does however
spread his forces dangerously thin.  

Rising activities of the Cult of Yurei are finally hitting home. Only now is the full impact of their plots and schemes being realised. The loss of Sagoya, now known as the City of the Damned, to the Cult was beyond the authorities’ wildest estimations and they are still, eight years on, reeling from its loss. Whilst it would never be admitted publicly in court, the rise of the Cult in the Sagoya swamps to the north east and the Eagle taking refuge in the jungles still further east has made this area of Jwar a no go area for the forces, and those friendly to the rulers, of the Prefecture of Ryu.  

The emerging threat of the Savage Wave would be a world shattering event at the best of times but these fractured times have allowed them to gain a foothold the demons could not have anticipated. Sages are quick to point out that this validates everything the monks of the Temple have been saying and that forces should be lent to support them in their cause. Hida is quick to dismiss their views however, as he is as devoted to Michi and the path of his ancestors as possible, a practice most of his family support.  

Despite these troubled times they remain in power and should not be underestimated by their enemies. The appearance of a living Ryu, Mizuchi, for the first time since the dark ages was heralded as a sign of the stirring of the Kami and in allying itself to the Takashi clan it swayed doubters to their cause. The Takashi clan used this evidence to cement their claim to the right to rule amidst the cacophony of counter claims, quelling dissenters at least for now, in and out of court. Another contributing factor to their success over the centuries is the famed Takashi discipline. Their troops fight with a unity and cohesion unmatched by many of the other clans, this solid organisation allows them to respond quickly to orders in battle and their ashigarus pride themselves on never breaking their lines in the face of even the most fearsome enemy charges. Organisation and excellent planning skills were executed during the war with the Eagle clan long before a katana was drawn in anger. Reserves were grown, resources stockpiled and alliances forged through methods that last longer than simply throwing moons at a situation. Marriage and positions for other clan’s heirs at the envied Takashi military school are amongst the strategies that have earned the Dragon lasting loyalty.  

The Takashi have never lost sight of the importance of gathering the support of the minor clans, from which they garner more support than any other major clan, often elevating a minor family, bringing them into the fold to further achieve the clan’s goals, an excellent example of this being the ascendancy of the Salamander clan in return for the knowledge of fire stone powder they had discovered. Their foresight has allowed the Takashi family to take and hold the Prefecture whilst maintaining a position of strength. It is said the Dragon is patient and remembers all.  

One of the first clans to adopt the practices of the forgotten Shugenja, members of the Takashi family such as Takashi Matsu are adept in manipulating the elements for the benefit of the clan. This, however, adds to the already strained enmity between the Takashi family and the Temple of Ro-Kan who believe the practice further weakens the barriers they created.  

The Dragon clan is, despite the difficulties it faces, the best hope Jwarians have to defend themselves against the coming storm. As enemy and ally alike know ‘the dragon is prepared’.

Practitioners of Shugenja with any real power are new to the world of the Jwar Isles, previously thought to be charlatans and conjurers of cheap tricks open to the ridicule of all.

Coinciding with the degradation of the Temple of Ro-Kan’s barriers, some rare individuals have been gifted with the ability to perform great feats. They have learnt to see the world as do the followers of the Temple but rather than striving to keep the balance they have twisted the elements that resonate within them to their own ends.  
This is in direct conflict with the teachings of Subarashī Baransu.

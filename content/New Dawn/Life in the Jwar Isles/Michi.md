The main religion practiced by the clans and peoples that inhabit the Jwar isles is that of Michi (The Path) which can best be described as a way of understanding and leading one’s life as opposed to a fixed religious institution. Michi teaches the pursuit of acceptance, truth and happiness through the achievement of one’s personal goals. Rather than there being a single deity, proponents of Michi believe that everyone can find the right path in their life if they can listen to the messages of their ancestors.  
These messages might come from within or be concealed in the world around them and it is the continual pursuit of understanding oneself in Michi that leads to a person following his path. This in turn leads to a person’s happiness and enlightenment.  

Although there are no strict places of worship, the Jwar Isles are covered in honden, shrines to the ancestors who guide us in life and try to keep us on our path.  
Each individual’s path is different and the belief is interpreted differently by many throughout the Isles and Empire. Acceptance of one’s current path is key to understanding that when we die if we have realised the truth of our existence then we pass through to Hantaisoku where we spend eternity guiding the generations through their Michi.  

When an individual comes of age, usually around thirteen, they are encouraged to learn about the different paths. For the noble classes there are three main routes:
- Samurai: The pursuit of enlightenment through the mastery of a skill, be that bow, sword or tactics.
- Shisai: The understanding of the messages from the ancestors and the mastery of communing with those of the past to enable the Shisai to better guide those around them on their paths for the future.
- Artisan: The mastery of a craft, whether it be the manipulation of an element, the written word, painting or people.

The major clans will often hold masters of various if not all paths. Lesser clans historically specialised in a certain path, the mastery of their chosen field increasing the likelihood
of an alliance with a clan of higher status. The Owl clan’s domination of the Shisai route allowed them to rise from relative obscurity to a position of true power, their advice has been heeded with close attention by Emperors for the last two hundred years.  
Those of the poorer classes tend to follow the paths of their parents.

The monks and followers of Subarashī Baransu believe that the world exists in balance, that life is a constant struggle to maintain the balance within ourselves and by extension our surroundings and the world at large. They believe that everything happens for a reason and in the context of this “Great Balance”. The expression of this can be seen in nature and is personified in the elements that make up the world and everything in it. The religion teaches its followers that even the characteristics of people and their actions can be understood through the elements and the six elements represent the driving emotions and characteristics that govern our actions.

- Fire: Passion, Anger and Action
- Metal: Determination and Persistence
- Water: Calm, Thoughtfulness and Flexibility
- Earth: Confidence and Stubbornness
- Air: Compassion, Wisdom and Growth
- Void: Life and Death

The followers believe that balance is essential and that the elements need to be kept in check to maintain this. They understand natural disasters such as tsunamis, earthquakes, volcanos, tornados and plague are the universe expressing imbalance.

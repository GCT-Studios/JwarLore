Led by Minimoto Hiroki, ‘The Old Bear’, of the Minimoto family. The Bear clan are a member of the Prefecture of Ryu although their loyalty to the Dragon clan is now coming under question. They again hold the title of ‘Stewards of the West’ bestowed upon them by the Dragon following the ‘Dragon Wars’. From Jyoto they control the north west of Jwar, supported by the wealth of the Iron Mountains.

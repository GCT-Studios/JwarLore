A stark contrast to the Pygmies, the Hill Tribesmen are a remarkably tall race though lean and rangy due to the arduous life the mountains of Jwar affords them. They have also a much more aggressive attitude to those they consider intruders in their lands and over the generations this has
often brought them into conflict with the clans. The wealth of the mountains they inhabit has meant that the clans are forced to run the risk of offending the various tribes.  

The clans would dismiss them as barbarians but in truth the tribes have a well structured society, tribal leaders gather annually with their tribes in tow to make wide ranging decisions for their people. Although observing the fighting and cavorting festivities that accompany these gatherings may raise doubts. This unity, however, has prevented the clans from simply eliminating them; their renowned prowess in battle helps as well of course.  

The Hill Tribesmen have long lived in harmonious symbiosis with the Tengu that make the mountains their home, the secrets of armed combat shared with those selected from the tribes have enabled them to defend what is theirs against any that have had the audacity to try and take it.

Led by Ikeda Nobu of the Ikeda family. The Peacock clan calls Kita home, its proximity to the city of Ryu has often meant that the clan has openly followed whichever clan is in power. However the marriage of the heir to the Ikeda clan to the daughter of ‘The Old Bear’ has secured support on the south coast for the Bear clan. It has also signalled lofty ambitions from the Peacock clan. It is notable that the Takashi family have recently recalled their family members from the cities Koryu.

Led by Tanaka Nobuo of the Tanaka family. Despite his unswerving loyalty to the Eagle, Nobuo however pained, bent the knee to the Dragon clan and maintained their position in the Prefecture.  
Unofficially it is believed that they still hold sympathies for the Shiho’s cause, possibly even lending support where possible. The Dragon is certainly wary of having an expected ally of an enemy so close, the Wolf's home city of Tsumaga lying just south west of Ryu.

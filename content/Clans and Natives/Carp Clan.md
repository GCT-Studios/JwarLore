Led by the ‘Three Storms’ triplets Satsuko, Shigeko and Shizuka of the Fujiwaro family. These three sisters rule the waters of the southern Jwar Isles and are renowned for their seafaring ability. They hold court from the largest of the port cities, Kanazawa. Found in their own archipelago, a series of relatively small islands that form the Spice Isles.

A diminutive people, the Pygmies have for generations lived isolated from the clans that have come and gone from their Isle of Izu. At home in the Shimogamo jungles they have lived a nomadic lifestyle, never staying in one place long enough to impact upon their fellow inhabitants.  
They have looked on from the edge of their lands with little comprehension as the clans have attempted to settle the flood lands of Izu.  

The arrival of the Ito has changed their lifestyle dramatically however. Most clans had dismissed the
Pygmies but the Mori family had quietly sought to understand them. Now however under the instruction of the Ito they hunt them down, years of building trust with these indigenous people has allowed them unique insight into the patterns of life they follow. The Mori attempted to persuade the Ito that the Pygmies were no threat but the Ito will not allow anything to jeopardize the security of the secrets of Orochi hidden away in the depths of the jungle.  
The newly appointed Jaguar clan have since learned that finding the Pygmies is much harder when they don’t want to be found.

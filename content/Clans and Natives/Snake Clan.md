Led by Ito Takuya of the Ito family. The Ito clan appeared to be fervent supporters of the Dragon clan during its rise to power. The clan has however gone through a transformation blessed upon them by their chosen Kami, Orochi, and they are now in open rebellion against the Prefecture. Their stronghold is the city of Okyo on the island of Izu.

Led by Suzuki Osamu of the Suzuki family. Whilst not technically a Jwarian clan they are included here as they hold swathes of land on the eastern edge of the Empire’s mainland directly across from the Jwar isles. The Swan clan is part of the Prefecture of Nara. The marriage of Takashi Hiro and the daughter of Osamu, preceded the Dragon Wars. The political interest of the Swan clan in the Jwar Isles is unknown.

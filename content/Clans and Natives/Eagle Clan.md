Led by Shiho Hiroto, ‘The Black Eagle’, their affiliation is to no one and any right to be considered a major clan was stripped from them by the Dragon clan. They are however a family steeped in history and old loyalties, and whilst not openly expressed in court, these alliances seemingly die hard. If any location was to be considered under their control it would be East Jwar although this is predominantly dense and uninhabitable jungle.

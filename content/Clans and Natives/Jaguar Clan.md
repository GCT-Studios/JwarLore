Led by Mori Yuuma of the Mori family. The Jaguar is a new clan that is not recognised by the Prefecture of Ryu, the Snake clan angered the other major clans when they granted clan status to the Mori family. On their ascent the Mori selected the Jaguar of the rainforests that they rely upon for their mon. Their loyalty to the Ito is unquestioning, they were amongst the last believers and upholders of Orochi before the Ito’s arrival on Izu.

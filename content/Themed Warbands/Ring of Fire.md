When Onisho come close to their time of ascension, ready to face their ring of fire, they are often put with other Onisho in the same situation and forced into battle after battle to trigger their instinctive response to challenge and fight to become Oni. Once grouped together in such a warband, the Onisho will fight until they become adult Oni or die… most will die, but it will be a glorious death and their soul will go on forever!  
Raw aggression and eternal combat for the Onisho in this themed warband. Play all out attack and go heedless of the damage knowing the quick rush from devouring souls will sustain your models until they crush their opposition.

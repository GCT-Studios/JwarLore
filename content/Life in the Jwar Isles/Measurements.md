Distance across the Jwar Isles is measured in leagues; this being the distance a man can walk in an hour, approximately four miles. The archipelago of islands that make up the Jwar Isles measure roughly five hundred and eighty leagues across, as the crane flies from ‘The Hook’ in the north east to the Spice Isles in the south west. Of these islands Jwar is the largest at four hundred and eighty leagues from ‘The Hook’ to ‘Atsuta’s Forest’.

The current year is 294 SK. SK or Saisho Kara (From The First) marks the transition from a period of time that is now known as Ankoku Jidai (The Dark Age). Year 0 SK began on the ascendancy of the first divine Emperor, Takawa Shiro. The lunar calendar was implemented and time has thus been recorded since. The uniting of the clans, creation of an Empire and Takawa Shiro’s rise to power is well chronicled in the tomes of the Blue Libraries of ‘Sen no Machi’ the capital city of the Empire and centre of the known world. A copy of which is also held by every clan of the Empire and yet the content still passes further, with each year, into the realm of myth and legend.  
The passage of time was not always measured so rigorously as it is now. In the Dark Age, Ankoku Jidai, it was measured not in years but in family history, people referring to the age of their father or their father’s father. This is remembered as a time of chaos and darkness across the lands. The old tales of beasts of the night roaming the land and yokai in their various guises preying on the divided clans all stem from this period.  
The 294 years of Empire have been mostly and certainly comparatively kind to the people and only those in the furthest reaches of the Jwar Isles still hold any resentment of swearing fealty to a boy Emperor thousands of leagues away on the mainland to the west.

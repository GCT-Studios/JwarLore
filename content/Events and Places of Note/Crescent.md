source: New Dawn rulebook
title: The Crescent
---
So named because of the shape the mountains form around the edge of Izu, making landing impossible but for a few beaches in the north west. The peaks of the mountain range feed the great lakes of Izu from which rivers flow to the valley floor and onwards to the sea. It is said that bathing in the great lakes of Izu grants long life and prosperity though few have returned to confirm these legends.

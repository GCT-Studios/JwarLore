Ataka sits at the crossroads of the central trade routes across Jwar. Jyoto to the north east sends all of its rich ore mined in the Iron Mountains directly to Ataka along the aptly named Iron Road. The trade houses of Ataka are bustling with traders looking to get a bargain before its goods are shipped on. Ataka is described in depth in the journals of Nido Yaki-Tobi.

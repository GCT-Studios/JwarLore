These training schools are prevalent across the Empire and Jwar boasts many successful schools. Within Jwar, the Koryu of Mikato has risen to prominence in recent times linked to the Takashi clan’s own rise to power and their tradition that all Takashi learn at the hands of Mikato’s masters. The Koryu, located in Kita is quickly becoming a destination for most wandering bushi hoping to improve their skills and one day master the Unblockable Strike.

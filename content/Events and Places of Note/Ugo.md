This medium sized town is located on the south west coast of Jwar. Once the home of the Jung clan, their unparalleled knowledge of the local atoll meant that they could transport an increasingly large amount of trade directly to the mainland, cutting out the ports of Ryu. A decision that would prove as costly as Fukata’s own. The Jung were brought to their knees and descended to piracy, no other has had the temerity to attempt to ship goods from Ugo since.

This garrison is located at the entrance to the only known Bakemono cave system and picked up its name from the soldiers stationed there. This outpost in the Iron Mountains is manned by the hardiest of the Minimoto, for those selected face regular onslaughts from the hordes of the Savage Wave. The Minimoto initially launched an attack on the beasts’ lair. They have not again. Long and narrow tunnels hindered the Minimoto whilst the Bakemono came into their own ferociously defending their homes.  

The Minimoto have discussed creating a rock fall to seal the entrance and prevent any more forays by the Savage Wave into the Bear’s land. They have come under pressure from the Prefecture of Ryu and even had word from the mainland that the entrance is not to be blocked ‘at any cost’.

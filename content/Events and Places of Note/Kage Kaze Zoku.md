Found somewhere in ‘The Hook’ mountain range, the exact location of the house of the Shadow Wind clan is a closely guarded secret. The clan’s estate is to be found, by those that know where to look, via a single cave. This path eventually opens onto a deep basin hidden away by sheer and, but for this one entrance, impenetrable mountain cliffs. Pockets of jungle like vegetation still cling audaciously to the basin’s walls and one such clump hides this entrance from those within. Only those successfully inducted into the clan ever learn of its presence. Far down on the basin’s floor in its centre is the fortress, of which they are so protective, surrounded by training grounds and at one edge a great lake fed by raging waterfalls. The process of induction into the clan is not fully known but it is believed that only orphans with no worldly ties are selected to join the clan.  
Training from childhood, few survive this gruelling process to adulthood and those that do are considered to be some of the world’s deadliest killers. Their unmatched skill and undying devotion to the Grand Master of the clan are a terrifying combination. The motives of the organisation are known only to the Grand Master, however it has not been unknown for major Jwarian clans to hire their unique services. Many wars have been ended before they started by the swift action of the clan’s ninja. It is important to note that the Kage Kaze Zoku is not recognised as a clan by the Empire. Its existence is tolerated only because there isn’t anything even the Emperor could do to stop it.

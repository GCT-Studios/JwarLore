The wars began fifteen years ago in the year 279 SK and lasted five years. The Takashi clan, the Dragon, led the Bear in an uprising against the Eagle of the Shiho. The Shiho fought valiantly in a series of battles but were unable to defend against the Bear clan from the Iron Mountains to the west and the Dragon from the east. The Shiho were overwhelmed by both this two pronged attack and treachery from their own allies and their last hope of support from the monks of the Ro-Kan to the north was met with only empty words of balance and peace. In a brutally final decision the combined forces under the Takashi clan crushed the Shiho completely. The Shiho were scattered and banned from mention in the courts of Jwar.

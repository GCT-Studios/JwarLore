The largest battle in the Dragon Wars. All the major clans of the Jwar Isles plus most of the minor
clans were involved. The army of the Eagle marched north west from Ryu, samurai and retainers formed from members of Shiho, Ito and many minor clans, were to unite with the forces of the Horse Lords at Ataka. En route they crossed paths with the Takashi clan army, made up of the Dragon, Carp and many others that rallied to the usurpers banners, just south of Hendai.  

The battle was going against the Takashi, the Shiho’s superior numbers proving decisive. The Takashi fell back to the streets of Hendai, followed relentlessly by the army of the Shiho. The battle however swung, as planned by the Takashi, when the Minimoto arrived in support. The brute force of the tetsubo favouring clan proved devastating in the close confines of the city streets. The
Shiho had no contingency plans as they had not expected to face the Minimoto. They were meant to be lost at sea on the Plains of Ataka, dispersed by the cavalry charge of the Horse Lords. When Takashi Hiroshi killed the Eagle general Shiho Hideo the battle was effectively over. This proved to be the turning point of the Dragon Wars and the Shiho found themselves on the back foot from here onwards.

The last major battle of the Dragon Wars. The Shiho garrison were defeated by the combined might of the Takashi, Minimoto, Carp and many more minor clans. However it takes more than overwhelming odds for Ryu to fall, the most important factor of all was the betrayal by the Ito clan, who seized control of both Ryu’s gates from within, opening the city to the enemies of the Shiho.

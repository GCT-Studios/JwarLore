Found in the north westerly corner of Jwar, Jyoto is the stronghold of the Minimoto clan, the ‘Stewards of the West’. This title was bestowed upon them by the Takashi clan for their support during the Dragon Wars. They are a clan steeped in the honour of service and are well known for being hardy. They fight with great gusto under the banner of the Bear.

This tree located in the most northerly point of ‘The Twilight Woods’ is considered most sacred by the monks of the Jwar Isles. Sitting in the centre of its own glade the ‘Old Grandfather’ is wider and taller than the surrounding trees as if they stand back respectfully bowing their canopied heads in reverence, allowing heaven’s light to shine upon its trunk. It is considered a great honour to sit and meditate in its presence.

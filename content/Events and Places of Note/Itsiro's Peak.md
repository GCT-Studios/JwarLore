This mountain range is named after its highest point, Itsiro’s Peak. Here in the clouds the monks of the Temple of Ro-Kan come to contemplate upon the element of Air. Itsiro’s Peak is also a pilgrimage site for those that follow Michi, here amongst the howling winds they believe they are closer to the voices of their ancestors and are able to realign themselves with their true path.

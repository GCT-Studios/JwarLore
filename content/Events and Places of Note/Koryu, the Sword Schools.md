A Koryu is a significant Swordfighting school. Only a Blademaster can found a new Koryu. Their students will carry on their techniques. Some clans have multiple Koryu (having had many Blademasters in their history), all but the newest or smallest clans have at least one.  

Koryu from the same clan are often very similar, for example the three Koryu for the Dragon Clan exemplify a similar fighting style but each has something of its own.  

The traditional Dragon Style (founded by Takashi Mikata) teaches the Ten-Uchi.  
Takashi Mako’s Koryu utilises the Iaijutsu and critical attack.  
Aiko now has the right to found a new school. His techniques are based on rote repetition of the basics to become flawless, rather than concerning a particular technique.  

The Ito’s Koryu (founder not named) uses larger weapons (no dachi) and powerful attacks, along with fast strikes and sidestepping.  Since their alliance with Orochi, this has been developed by Itsunagi (though he has yet to formally found a new Koryu) with the addition of combination attacks (sometimes utilising two katana) and the technique from Kurouma Koryu.  
These additions will likely be part of Itsunagi’s Koryu if he ever founds it.  

> [!NOTE]
Itsunagi visited all the 8 living Blademaster’s Koryu throughout the empire to learn all techniques.  Most of them consider him to be the best swordsman alive.

Minimoto Koryu (founder not named) emphasises attack only. There are no defences taught. Why bother to defend if an opponent cannot hurt you?  
The offense is mostly based on powerful attacks but they practice any attacking manoeuvre except those involving movement. The style is very static, usually using a bloody great club.  

The Shiho Koryu was destroyed. The building and all students were killed. Now only a few old men can barely practice it, and over-eager youngsters try to live up to a heritage they can never match, learning with substandard weapons in the swamps and wilderness.  
Hiroto’s return and his “unique” take on the Shiho style could be called a new Koryu, utilising the Shiho’s famous stabbing technique (notoriously difficult to perform with a curved blade) and his hard-won experience duelling in the fighting pits and fighting for his life as an outlaw.  
Shiho Koryu now expounds unpredictability.  By using techniques the opponent is unprepared for, an inferior fighter can gain an advantage over a veteran.  

Kurouma Koryu is no longer taught. From a fallen house, a few in the Jwarr Isles studied under the last master but the whole house is now gone and the style is considered cursed by some.  
This style utilised an odd quirk of its founding house: they were all left handed. The style has techniques for left-handed samurai and also ambidextrous samurai who can wield two katana.  
Other houses often sent left handed samurai to learn and the house gained prestige for this. Ito Itsunagi and Takashi Hagane both learned there.  

There is no actual Jung School or a single teacher but the family do pass on their skills and their style could be referred to as the Jung Koryu.  

The Shadow Wind houses also have their own unique styles and masters but obviously nobody knows who they are or where they teach their deadly fighting techniques.

Found in the ‘Iron Mountains’, so named for the rusty reddish tinge the surface present iron ore provides. The steep and intimidating pass walls tower into the sky and make passing through a daunting prospect. The Minimoto clan are eager to start pressing ahead and start accessing the readily available minerals. However, they have met staunch and unexplainable resistance from the Hill Tribesmen of the mountains, resulting in more than a few low level skirmishes; the pass starting to live up to its name for the wrong reasons.

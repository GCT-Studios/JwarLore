The forest gets its name from the deep blue flower that seems to flourish here. This, coupled with the thick canopy of the age old forest, creates a sombre and melancholy feeling in those travelling through. It is said that the spirits of those who have lost a loved one linger here. It is their grief that causes the flowers to grow and that if one stays here too long they will lie down and never get up, taken by a deep and unshakable sadness, simply losing the will to live.
